\# Assignment 1 Reflection



\## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?



The breakthrough was realising that a mathematically correct visualisation is not necessarily a good explanation. My first prototype worked correctly: the slider changed the queue and waiting time, the mathematical model was consistent, and the tests passed. However, when I viewed the page in the browser, a single blue dot did not clearly look like a queue, and the curve showed the result without clearly explaining its meaning.



This changed how I approached the next iterations. Instead of simply adding more text or features, I focused on making the interaction itself explain the idea. I added clearer queue flow, near-capacity feedback, and spare capacity so that users could understand not only that waiting time increases near 100% utilisation, but also why the system becomes fragile.



\## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?



This project changed how I think about working with coding agents. I do not want to use an agent by repeatedly asking it to fix individual outputs until something looks acceptable. I want to define better rules, tests, and constraints that guide the agent toward better results.



Updating `CLAUDE.md` after discovering problems showed me that improving the environment the agent works within can be more valuable than writing another prompt. I want to become a developer who uses agents deliberately, verifies their output, and improves the process as well as the final product.
